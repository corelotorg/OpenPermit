# Internet Archive Terms of Use: not captured

Checked 2026-09-28. https://archive.org/about/terms.php renders with JavaScript; the fetched
HTML is an empty application shell and the Wayback Machine copy is the same. The terms text
was therefore not captured (the empty shell is kept outside the repository at
$ORI_CORPORA/licenses/ia-terms-of-use.html with its SHA-256 in the manifest).

The public-domain basis of each Internet Archive item used by the study does not rest on the
Internet Archive's site terms. It rests on the item's own rights facts (metadata captured in
this folder as ia-<identifier>-metadata.json from https://archive.org/metadata/<identifier>/metadata)
and on US law (17 U.S.C. 105 for federal works; the pre-1931 term rules, see usc-17-304.txt,
us-copyright-office-circular-15a.txt and cornell-public-domain-chart-2026.txt).
UNVERIFIED: the Internet Archive's own terms of use.
