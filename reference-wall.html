REFERENCE ASSEMBLIES · PUBLIC WORKING DRAFT

From a wall to a verifiable permit claim.

One small synthetic stud-wall fixture demonstrates the difference between geometry verification, regulatory applicability, IFC interoperability and an authorized decision.

Scope: geometry-contract example only. Code applicability OPEN · approval OPEN · IFC exchange NOT INCLUDED. No construction or professional certification is claimed.

What the public example includes

Parametric specimen

48 × 96 in model, nominal 2×4 members at 16 in centers, 7/16 in OSB and stable point identifiers.

Deterministic rejection

52 synthetic fastener-location candidates; validator rejects identity shifts, missing points and fabricated PASS gates.

Typed evidence boundary

Sources, jurisdiction, geometry, IFC and human authority are distinct attestations.

Interoperability and next promotion gates

IFC4X3, STEP, OpenCascade and GLB are useful in different roles: semantic building exchange, geometry interchange, exact geometry kernel and visual runtime projection. No standard is a proxy for code compliance.

Prior internal experimentation covered more complex wall openings, connections, IFC identity round-trips and evidence plates. That work has not been released here. Publishing those implementations requires a source-rights review, independent parser check, complete manifest, negative tests and an external checker receipt.

Research and Fast Footing

Virginia permitting vocabulary, source provenance and minimal single-footing approval are research/pilot workstreams. They require jurisdiction-specific authority and a lawful publication determination before any standard or claim is promoted.
