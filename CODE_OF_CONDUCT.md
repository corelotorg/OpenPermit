# Code of conduct

ORI is a public technical commons. This code applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests,
discussions and any other project space, and to anyone acting on behalf of the project elsewhere.

## Expected

- Argue from evidence: sources, fixtures, tests and reproducible results.
- Challenge claims, not people. A challenge record is a normal part of ORI, not an attack.
- Be precise about authority. Say whether something is law, guidance, interpretation or ORI's own
  assertion.
- Respect people's time and privacy. Keep personal data out of public records.
- Credit others' work where it is due.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background or
  ability.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.
- Misrepresenting endorsement, affiliation or authority on behalf of a person, government or
  organization.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers through the repository's private reporting
channel described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), marking the report "conduct". Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, suspend or ban
participants, in proportion to the harm. Decisions and their reasons are recorded for the person
affected.
